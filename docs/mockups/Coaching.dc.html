4 · Fitness, fatigue and form
Weekly load. A jump over about 30% shows amber; a spike with a pain flag shows red. Behind it are three numbers: fitness (long-term load), fatigue (short-term load) and form (the gap), the same idea as TrainingPeaks' performance chart. Load per run uses distance, time and heart rate where available, otherwise effort times minutes.
