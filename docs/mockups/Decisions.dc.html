Decisions and build order

All six earlier recommendations stand, plus four structure decisions from our last rounds. Below: the build order and a realistic 15-day path for the web version, then Android.

1Where it lives
Decided
Wix first, director app later
Phase 1 runs entirely on Wix (fastest, and the expo desk is already there). A separate director app for budget and rosters comes in Phase 2.
2Registration and payments
Decided
Keep today's registration, import runners
Admin view and findmybib sync on top. Owning payments waits until Phase 3.
3One race or many
Decided
Many races from day one
VHM, Ultribe, Time Trials; copy a race to next year.
4Who gets a login
Decided
Four roles
Director, team lead per module, desk or station volunteer, read-only sponsor.
5Messages
Decided
Email plus WhatsApp
Needs a business messaging provider; WhatsApp approval can take days, so start it on day 1.
6Only VTRA or a product
Decided
Keep the door open
Separate data per organiser, branding later.
7Structure
Decided
Four zones
Race, Community, Coach and More features, as on every screen's menu.
8Training plans
Decided
Free plan one way, coached plan two way
Free: [X] weeks, no messaging. Coached: paid, coach messages, feedback, tracking.
9Where pacers and volunteers sit
Decided
Pacers in Community, volunteers in Race
Volunteers are rostered per event. Pacers are recruited like ambassadors.
10Coach platform
Decided
Separate product
Own login, own pricing, runs all year, not tied to one race.

15-day web plan (my estimate)
Days 1-2Lock the scope. You give me the inputs listed below; I set up the collections and logins.
Days 3-6Volunteers: shifts, sign-up link, roster, reminders.
Days 7-9Command centre and race-day control with incident log.
Days 10-11Pacers roster, QR check-in, safety checklist.
Days 12-13Rehearsal with real VHM data on Wix, with your team on the desks.
Day 14Fix what the rehearsal shows. Freeze changes.
Day 15Go live.
This holds only if inputs arrive in the first two days and your team is free for the rehearsal. The estimate is mine, not a guarantee; real Wix behaviour has surprised us before and the expo package has not yet run on the live site.
Android (Phase 2)
Week 1 to 2 after web: make the web app installable on phones, with camera for selfies and QR.
8 to 12 weeks part-time: a real Android app for runners and coaches, then Play Store review.
New personal Google Play developer accounts may need a period of closed testing before release; check this when you create the account.
What I need from you
• Race date, and the VHM bib and size columns from findmybib
• What registration tool you use today
• WhatsApp business provider choice, and payment gateway for later
• The free-plan length [X], coached-plan price and coach count
• Names for the volunteer roles and shifts
• Your own setup key for the expo package
About the free Claude plan: it has usage limits and I have already hit one session limit in this project. Work in short, focused tasks and keep a spare day. If limits block you near go-live, the plan needs a paid tier or a developer helping.
