Training: free plans and coached plans

Every registered runner gets a free starter plan for a set number of weeks, delivered one way. Runners who want a coach move to a coached plan with two-way messaging, tracking and feedback. Coaches have their own console.

Runner onboarding (the steps TrainingPeaks coaches use)
1 · AccountCreated from the race registration; no second sign-up
2 · ProfileGoal race, recent result, days free, injuries to note
3 · DevicesConnect Garmin, Strava or Polar; avoid duplicate uploads
4 · ZonesEstimate pace and heart-rate zones from a recent race; refine after a test
5 · PlanPick the free plan or request a coach
6 · CalendarChoose how many days ahead to see; add travel notes
7 · RulesAgree what to write after each run and the reply time
8 · Load basicsShort explainer on training load and recovery
Injury notes are visible only to the runner and the assigned coach.
